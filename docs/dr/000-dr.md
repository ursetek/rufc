# Use URSE DR

Status: **ACCEPTED**

## Context

The project requires a formal method for documenting decisions. Without
this, it is difficult to track the rationale behind specific decisions,
leading to their reconsideration or misunderstanding.

## Decision

We will use
[URSE DR](https://ursetek.github.io/standards/standards/dr/index.html) to
document all decisions.

## Consequences

*   Each decision will have its own DR.
*   DRs will be stored in `docs/dr/` in Markdown format.
*   DRs will be numbered and have a status (Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated).
